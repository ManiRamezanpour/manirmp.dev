## The problem

The shape former is a lab rig: a stepper motor drives the forming head while a load
cell measures the force. The goal was to run cycles from a browser instead of a
serial monitor on a laptop wired to the Arduino — and to keep readings trustworthy
as the rig warms up.

## Decisions worth mentioning

- **Two codebases, one contract.** The Arduino only understands newline-terminated
  text commands. That kept the firmware dumb and testable from any serial monitor,
  and let the web side evolve independently.
- **A background serial reader.** `SerialManager` runs a reader thread that keeps the
  last 2,500 lines in a ring buffer, and `wait_for()` blocks until a matching reply
  arrives. Flask requests never block on a half-read line.
- **Temperature compensation in firmware.** Strain gauges drift with heat, so a
  DS18B20 sits next to the load cell and every reading is divided by
  `1 + 0.0002 × (T − 25 °C)` — the calibration temperature — before it leaves the
  board.
- **Develop off-device.** `pyserial` is optional and the port comes from
  `SERIAL_PORT`, so the whole UI runs on a laptop (or Vercel) without hardware,
  then deploys unchanged to the Pi.
